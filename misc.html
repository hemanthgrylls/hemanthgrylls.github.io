Poetry

Can you remain still?

When the clouds turned gray
When the heart overwhelmed with grayest grim
When it was no one's fault
While the deep cuts altered your senses
Can you remain still?
Can you let the rain fall on your face?

Do you have it?
The stillness it takes
For the mud to settle
Till it's clear

Can you remain still?
Can you remain still?
Can you empty yourself for the right thing to unfold itself?

HC (inspired by Taoism), Sep 20th 2026
